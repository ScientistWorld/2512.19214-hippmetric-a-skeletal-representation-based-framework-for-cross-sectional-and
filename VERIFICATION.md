# Verification Report

## Initial Assessment

The workspace entered final verification claiming `core_claim`. The implementation, baseline, reusable evaluator, score files, train/test split, and documentation were already present. I treated the milestone as valid unless a concrete integrity issue appeared.

`python validate.py --compare` passes, including reference/scores schema checks, method/evaluation import separation, train/test evaluator wiring, absence of committed `shared/` references, and method-key consistency across train/test scores.

## Issues Found

- **Minor**: Ignored generated artifacts from previous jobs were still present in the working tree (`__pycache__`, generated job artifacts, and a core file). These were not part of the research gym interface.
- **Cosmetic/system**: `README.md` and `manifest.json` remain modified because they are busy system-managed placeholder files in this environment. `git restore` cannot replace them, and they are unrelated to the gym contents.

No critical issues were found. The scored metrics are scientific quantities rather than wall-clock speed or deviation from paper values, and the method-agnostic files do not expose the paper's implementation details beyond legacy metric field names required by the score schema.

## Fixes Applied

- Removed generated bytecode, core dump, and transient job artifacts where the filesystem allowed it.
- Confirmed `scripts/download.sh` is self-contained for this procedural benchmark and contains no `shared/` dependency.
- Confirmed `eval/` and `data/` do not import from `method/`.
- Confirmed `scoring/EXPERIMENTS.md` matches the experiments in `scoring/scores.json` and remains method-agnostic.
- Left the implementation and scoring definitions unchanged because the current scores validate the core claim.

## Final Judgment

The workspace is ready to serve as a research gym. Improve-mode agents can run and modify the existing method, and from-scratch agents can use the problem, data, and evaluator without depending on the paper implementation. The main limitation is that the benchmark uses a procedural synthetic substitute rather than the unavailable clinical/manual datasets from the paper, but the baseline is not saturated and the method shows the claimed correspondence and morphometry gains.
