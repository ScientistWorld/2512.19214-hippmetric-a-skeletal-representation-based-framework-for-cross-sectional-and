# Train/Test Evaluation Design Notes

Current design (final hardening iteration; see `HARDENING.md`).

- **Interface.** Every `scripts/predict_<name>.sh INPUT_NPZ OUTPUT_NPZ` on disk is a method, called once per cohort. Inputs are binary segmentations (format in `data/hippo_io.py`; `cohort` = `procedural` or `real` selects the template); outputs are, per scan and per template skeletal point (61 x 9 grid), the skeletal point and the superior/inferior spoke endpoints. Nothing else a method writes is read.
- **Procedural cohort.** Train: `data/hippo_train.npz` + `data/hippo_train_labels.npz` (32 subjects x 4). Test: generated by `eval/test/hidden_cohort.py` at evaluation time with a seed that exists only in `eval/test/evaluate.py`.
- **Real-anatomy cohort.** Baselines are MSD Task04 manual hippocampus labels: 228 development cases in `data/hippo_real_anatomy.npz` (train uses 32 of them), 32 held-out cases only in `eval/test/real_heldout.npz`. Follow-ups are generated at evaluation time (`eval/{train,test}/real_cohort.py`, separate files, separate seeds): erosion of the head along the true normals (1-2 anisotropic bumps, growing over time), an independent smooth multi-scale warp per follow-up, a new pose, and segmentation noise. Scoring follows each method's own baseline endpoints through the known change (longitudinal correspondence inside/outside the atrophic region, thickness change of each spoke), plus implied-surface RMS and validity.
- **Templates.** Procedural: regenerated inside `eval/test/` for the gate. Real: `data/hippo_real_template.npz` (built by `data/build_real_template.py` from the 228 development labels); the test gate uses its fixed copy `eval/test/real_template.npz`.
- **Recomputed metrics.** All metrics are computed from the raw coordinates; the real-anatomy metrics are filed under the three experiments (thickness change + validity, correspondence, surface).
- **Failure rule.** Crash, timeout (2 h per cohort call), missing/malformed/non-finite/absurd output, validity < 0.5 in either cohort, or no better than an evaluator-side trivial reference (constant thickness, no change, template at the centroid) => worst value in every scored metric of every experiment.
- **Data provenance.** Procedural files: `python eval/test/hidden_cohort.py --export-public data` (development seed 20251219). Real files: `bash scripts/download.sh` (MSD archive) then `python data/build_real_template.py` and `python eval/test/build_real_heldout.py`.
